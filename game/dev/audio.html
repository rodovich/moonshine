Checks

Levels renders everything offline at full volume and measures it; peaks are before the safety clipper, and anything above −1 dBFS is flagged. Balance plays each voice of each section alone and measures its A-weighted level; the melody should lead every other voice by 3 dB or more. Spectrum shows each track's energy by band, relative to its total. Lint checks the scores against their chords (docs/audio-direction.md, "Checking the music").
