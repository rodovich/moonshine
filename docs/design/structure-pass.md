# Structure pass: Acts 2 to 5

This is the approved design for a structural pass across Acts 2 to 5, made after client feedback. The reviewer (4Zv_hz69Qu) verified it, and the orchestrator approved it at f310bf1. Its decisions are folded into the [story bible](../story-bible.md), [01](01-opening.md) to [06](06-moon-in-the-bowl.md), and the [overview](overview.md); where an act document and this one disagree, this one wins until the builders' "As built" notes catch up.

## The proposal in brief

The client played to about Act 4 and found the game thinner and more linear than intended (their notes are quoted in full at the end). The cause is mostly Act 2: it has two threads, one of which is "give someone your hat," and most of the people the player meets there have nothing to do until Act 4 or 5. The fixes:

1. **Act 2 gets three required threads instead of two,** and five more characters get jobs in them. The Counting Woman now needs to see the "ninety-nine" in print *twice* before she storms off. One printed source is the guidebook, which now comes through a chain of trades between characters (the Lighthouse Keeper's telescope, the Shopkeeper's window, the Tourist) instead of Cheryl's own hat. The other is the Tide-Sheet, the newspaper on Lamp Street, which the Man on Bench can't read because his wife is wearing his glasses in her hair.
2. **The telescope comes back in Act 5.** The Lighthouse Keeper gave it away because it kept pointing at the sea. After the reveal, she wants it back to watch the reef, and she lets Cheryl use the lamp in return. This replaces the brass polish and gives her a small arc across the game.
3. **Ottilie becomes the hub of Act 4.** Reading the Ship Plan, she now points at four concrete things: the bell, the Fountain, Mott's copper, and the Skipping Girls.
4. **Signposting:** a staged beat and a sound mark the hollow step once the Counting Woman leaves; the Square gets new lines that point back to it; the Counting Woman's first reaction names the other printed source; the paper and charcoal come with a clear instruction ("look down"); the Harbor is reached by walking through the Bilge, and the Square points east to it.
5. **Geography:** the Goat Path becomes a natural cliff path three screens tall, taller than the Hundred Steps, and it comes down the west side of the island (the Back) into the west end of the Bilge, not into the Harbor. The Harbor stays east of the Bilge, so the map's left and right agree everywhere. The Harbor becomes a spur off the loop, so the Square and the Shop send the player there.
6. **Cheryl's own hat is retired.** The Shop's hat is a new item; old saves lose Cheryl's hat back to the wardrobe.

Everything else stands: Act 1 (apart from the hat), Act 3's shape, Act 4's three threads, Act 5's three threads, and Act 6.

- **The Keeper**, on the second talk or after Cheryl has looked at the telescope:

  - Cheryl uses the telescope on the window display herself: she sets the telescope in, and lifts the hat out. "In goes the telescope. Out comes the hat. The window is still complete."

### Retiring Cheryl's hat

- **A new item, `shop_hat`** ("Straw Hat"): "A wide straw hat, out of the Shop window. Display stock, retired." Declines: "It's for the Tourist. She's peeling." on anyone else; the Shopkeeper: "No returns, Miss. The window's complete."
- **Cheryl's `hat` is retired.** It's no longer taken in the opening's morning; the wardrobe keeps its look. Every line that assumes the Tourist has Cheryl's hat changes to the straw hat, and every decline for `hat` goes. In the build: square.js (the Shopkeeper's "That's YOUR hat"), archive.js (Ottilie), harbor.js (Mott), act5/town.js, and the opening's flat. In the docs: 01 (the wardrobe, the hat's item row, its declines), 02, 05 (the Tourist's "I'm wearing your hat"; the fallen window with "the hat on the floor" and its art), 06 (the Tourist's "I'm keeping your hat"), and the story bible (the Tourist, Shopkeeper, and Keeper rows and the Act 2 summary).
- **Old saves.** Two rules run on load:

  - The Tourist's later lines don't name whose hat it was ("I'm keeping this hat."), so they read right either way.
  - For `telescope.migrated` saves only, the Act 5 Keeper's second line is: LIGHTHOUSE KEEPER: And my telescope's in a SHOP WINDOW. Don't ask me how.

## 5. Geography

### The rule

The story bible already sets it: north is the Crown, south is the Harbor, **the Back is the steep west end**, and the Prow (the lighthouse) points southeast. The Goat Path's top is right (it leaves Lamp Street by the west gate), but its bottom isn't: it comes out at the Harbor, which is east of the Bilge. The proposal moves the bottom, not the top, because the west side also has Mott's shack and the sea cave, and the Goat Path's own lines already say "You can't see the Gate from here" (the Gate is east).

### The Goat Path, rebuilt

- **16 × 42 tiles, three screens tall.** It drops from Lamp Street to the Bilge, so it's the Steps' height and then some.
- **A natural cliff path:** bare rock, scree fans, gorse and wild thyme, goat-worn ground, a few rough cut steps where it's steep. No garden beds; the only built things are Mott's shack and its yard wall.
- **Irregular switchbacks, not a zigzag ladder.** The legs differ in length; one bends around a rock outcrop; one is only half the map's width; the post-and-rope rail appears only where there's a real drop.
- **Walk time:** about 25–30 seconds from top to bottom at walking pace. The loop is walked several times in Act 2 and again in Act 4 for Mott, so the builder should time it and shorten legs if it runs long.
- **Height cues:** at the top, gulls wheel *below* the path and the lower town's roofs are small at the east edge; halfway, a viewpoint at a hairpin looks out west over the open Bowl to the western Rim; near the bottom, the roofs are at eye level and the sea is close. Cheryl's first line at the top: "The Goat Path. Down the back of the island the long way. The Steps and then some, plus goats."
- **The top** is entered from Lamp Street's west gate at the top right of the map, facing left, so the player keeps walking the way they were going.
- **On the way:** Mott's shack partway down (its Act 4 state stays as designed); the sea cave mouth near the bottom, at the waterline (the Act 4 Sea Cave map is unchanged).
- **The bottom** leaves by the east edge onto the **west end of the Bilge's south quay** (the flooded paving in Act 2).
